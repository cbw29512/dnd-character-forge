Character Forge FAQ

Questions about the D&D 5e pregen generator.

Quick answers about what Character Forge supports, what it deliberately does not guess, and how generated characters are saved and printed.

What is a D&D pregen?

A pregen is a character prepared before play so a player can get to the table quickly. Character Forge generates ready-to-play characters from its supported rules surface instead of requiring every choice to be assembled manually.

Does Character Forge support both 2014 and 2024?

Yes. The Forge has separate 2014 · SRD 5.1 and 2024 · SRD 5.2.1 modes and is designed to keep edition-specific mechanics isolated.

What levels can it generate?

The current supported production matrix covers levels 1–20 across all 12 supported SRD classes.

Does it include every D&D book option?

No. Character Forge intentionally describes its scope as supported SRD content, plus clearly labeled Character Forge Original options. Unsupported book content is not silently invented.

What happens when I leave a choice Random?

The Forge resolves that choice from the supported options available for the selected rules edition. Random does not bypass validation or expand the source boundary.

Can I choose spells?

For supported spellcasting characters, the Forge includes a spell-choice workflow. You can fix supported spell choices you care about and leave remaining legal choices Random.

Can I save generated pregens?

Yes. Saved Pregens are kept locally in your browser. The Pregen library also supports user-initiated backup export/import and duplicate protection.

Does Character Forge require an account?

No. The current application does not require an account or hosted character database. See the Privacy page for the current local-storage model.

Can I print the character?

Yes. Character Forge produces class-specific US Letter print/PDF packets with Deluxe and Ink Saver presentation modes.

Can I use my own character portrait?

Yes. The current app supports user-supplied portrait replacement as well as class-specific fallback portraits.

What if a combination is unsupported?

The accuracy contract is fail-closed: unsupported combinations and unresolved required supported rules references should be rejected rather than guessed.

Is Character Forge affiliated with Wizards of the Coast?

No. Character Forge is an independent third-party project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Wizards of the Coast.
