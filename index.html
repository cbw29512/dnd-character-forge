ABOUT CHARACTER FORGE

A free D&D 5e pregenerated character generator for 2014 and 2024 SRD rules

Character Forge creates complete level 1–20 pregenerated characters for supported D&D 5e SRD classes. Pick the edition, class, species, background, spells, or class options you care about—or leave them Random—and the Forge completes the rest without crossing rulesets.

Each supported RAW result is validated before display and can be exported as a premium, class-aware printable character sheet with a rules audit. Unsupported rules content is deliberately unavailable instead of being approximated.

Character Forge FAQ

Does Character Forge support both 2014 and 2024 D&D 5e rules?

Yes. Character Forge keeps the 2014 SRD 5.1 and 2024 SRD 5.2.1 rulesets isolated so a generated character follows one edition from level 1 through 20.

Are generated characters rules-validated?

Yes. The Forge validates supported RAW characters before display and includes a printable rules audit with the character's SRD source boundary.

Can I generate a character without choosing every option?

Yes. Leave any supported choice set to Random and Character Forge fills every remaining choice with a legal option while preserving the selections you fixed.

Can I print the generated character sheet?

Yes. Validated characters include premium printable, class-aware character sheets designed for US Letter output.
